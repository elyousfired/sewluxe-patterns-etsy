## Part 1: Universal Printing, Layers & Projector Guide PDF
Deliverable: `How-to-Print-and-Use-PDF-Patterns.pdf` (6 pages, `ReportLab`, Elsie house style `#9C4A57` & `#262322`, included in every Etsy listing).

### Page-by-Page Structure:
1. **Page 1 — Cover & Quick-Start Checklist**:
   - Title: `HOW TO DOWNLOAD, PRINT & ASSEMBLE YOUR PDF PATTERN`.
   - The Golden Rule Box: **Always open and print from free Adobe Acrobat Reader on a laptop or desktop computer** (never print from a phone, tablet, or web browser preview window, as browsers ignore scale and layers).
2. **Page 2 — Using Multi-Size Layers & Checking Scale**:
   - Step-by-step diagram showing the **Layers icon (stacked squares)** on the left sidebar of Adobe Acrobat Reader.
   - How to uncheck all sizes except the buyer's size (and keep the `Base / Legend` layer checked).
   - Print dialog settings: Select **`Actual Size` or `Custom Scale: 100%`** (NEVER `Fit to Printable Area` or `Shrink Oversized Pages`).
   - **Test Square Check**: Print Page 1 first and measure the **`4 in × 4 in (101.5 mm × 101.5 mm)`** calibration square with a rigid ruler.
3. **Page 3 — Assembling A4 & US Letter Tiled Sheets**:
   - Clear vector diagram showing how tile numbers work (`1/1, 2/1, 3/1...` grouped by the second number into rows, or `r/c` row/column grid depending on the file).
   - Time-saving tip: Trim only the **right** and **bottom** margins of each sheet, align the border lines and corner diamonds, and tape with matte tape (can be ironed on low heat without melting).
4. **Page 4 — How to Grade Between Two Sizes**:
   - Illustrated guide for sewists whose measurements fall across two sizes (e.g., Bust = Size 8, Waist = Size 12, Hips = Size 14):
     - Turn ON both size layers in Adobe Acrobat (`Size 8` + `Size 12`) and print together.
     - Draw a smooth diagonal transition line connecting the Size 8 bust line to the Size 12 waist line using a French curve or ruler.
     - Blend seam allowances and notches smoothly.
5. **Page 5 — A0 Copyshop & Projector Sewing Setup**:
   - **A0 / Wide Format**: What to tell a local print shop or online pattern printer ("Print at 100% scale on 36"×48" or A0 bond paper, black & white or color").
   - **Projector Setup**: How to open the Projector PDF in Adobe Acrobat, enter Full Screen (`Ctrl+L` / `Cmd+L`), turn on the `1 inch` or `5 cm` Calibration Grid layer, zoom until the projected grid matches a cutting mat on the table, and toggle the desired size layer.
6. **Page 6 — Lengthening / Shortening & Need Help?**:
   - Diagram showing how to slash-and-spread (to lengthen) or fold-and-pin (to shorten) bodice, sleeve, or pants pieces cleanly at mid-section while truing the side seam.
   - Warm contact card: *"Stuck on a step or unsure which size to cut? Send us a message on Etsy with your Bust, Waist, and Hip measurements—we're always happy to help!"*

## Part 2: Etsy Customer Support & Diagnostic Playbook
When the user pastes a customer message from Etsy, diagnose the root cause against the pattern file and output a ready-to-send English reply (plus a short explanation in the user's language):

### Common Scenarios & Exact Fixes:
1. **"My 4x4 inch test square measures 3.75 inches / 9.5 cm!"**
   - *Cause*: Printed from Chrome/Safari/Edge browser preview or Mac Preview with "Scale to Fit" enabled.
   - *Fix*: Give exact 3-step instructions to open the file in free Adobe Acrobat Reader and select `Actual Size / 100%`.
2. **"I can't download the files on my iPhone / Etsy App!"**
   - *Cause*: The Etsy mobile app disables digital file downloads.
   - *Fix*: Explain gently that Etsy requires digital downloads to be opened via a mobile web browser (`Safari`/`Chrome` at `etsy.com/your/purchases`) or a computer, OR offer to email/send the direct Google Drive link right inside the Etsy chat so they have zero friction.
3. **"Which size should I make? My bust is X, waist is Y, hips are Z."**
   - *Action*: Check the exact garment's Body Chart AND Finished Garment Measurements (ease). Tell the buyer which primary measurement governs that garment (Bust for jackets/tops/dresses; Waist & Hips for pants/skirts), recommend the exact US + Letter size, state how much wearing ease they will have, and explain if they should blend between two sizes.
4. **"Step N is confusing / How do I attach the collar/facing/slit?"**
   - *Action*: Check the booklet step, write a crystal-clear 3-bullet walkthrough in plain language, and attach a zoomed annotated diagram + the direct YouTube tutorial timestamp.
